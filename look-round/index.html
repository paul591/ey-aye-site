Step one

The look-round.

An hour at your place, watching how the work actually flows. You get a straight aye or nay on where AI would save real time in your business and where it'd just be a toy. No slides, no pitch.

What happens

  • We come to you, at a time the business is actually running, not a quiet Friday afternoon.
  • We watch the admin: the bookings, the quotes, the chasing, the screens you click round. We ask what's eating the mornings.
  • We look at what you're already paying for (most businesses have an AI subscription nobody's using properly).
  • Within two days you get one page: three or four ayes with a rough cost and a rough saving, and the nays with the reason.

What you leave with

  • A clear idea of whether any of this is worth your money. Sometimes the answer's nay, and we'll say so.
  • Usually one thing you can do yourself that afternoon with the tools you've already got.
  • No obligation. Roughly half of look-rounds turn into a half-day; the rest got a useful hour.

Who it's for

Any business in Harrogate, Knaresborough, Ripon, Wetherby or within an hour's drive, from a one-person clinic to a sixty-person workshop. If you're bigger than that or outside the area, the fractional AI lead page is probably the one you want.
