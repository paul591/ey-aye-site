# Ey-aye image prompts

For Midjourney, Flux, Ideogram or similar. Paste the **base style** once, then one **subject** line per image.
Real photographs of you and real premises always beat these; use generated images only where you don't have the real thing yet.

## Base style (prepend to every prompt)

> Documentary-style photograph of a small business in Harrogate, North Yorkshire. Natural daylight, slightly overcast, soft shadows. Honest and unglamorous: real worn surfaces, a mug of tea, paperwork, an ordinary laptop. Colour palette leans warm stone and off-white (#F3F0EA) with one deep teal object (#0F6E6A) and one small mustard-yellow accent (#F2C230) somewhere in frame. 35mm lens, eye level, shallow but not dramatic depth of field, muted Kodak Portra tones. No people's faces in focus. No robots, no glowing screens, no circuit boards, no blue holograms, no stock-photo smiles. Looks like a real Tuesday.

Negative prompt (where supported): `robot, hologram, neon, glowing brain, circuit board, futuristic, 3D render, cartoon, stock photo, perfect teeth, lens flare, dramatic lighting, text, watermark`

Aspect ratios: hero 16:9 (export 1600×900), blog thumbnail 16:10 (1280×800), Open Graph 1200×630, home-page hero portrait 4:5.

## Van and social

## Rules

- One teal object and one yellow accent per image, no more. If it looks branded, it's too much.
- Never a face in focus unless it's really Paul.
- Never a screen showing anything legible that could be mistaken for a real product. Blur or keep it generic.
- Every image should pass the test: "could this have been taken in Harrogate this morning?"
