How we work. Five steps, no surprises.

Most people stop after step two and that's fine. Nobody gets a three-month discovery phase, a slide deck, or an account manager who's never seen their business. You deal with the person doing the work, start to finish.

1

The look-round

An hour at your place. We watch how the work actually flows, ask daft questions, and look at the screens you're clicking round all day. You get a one-page list: three or four ayes (worth doing, roughly what it'd cost and save), and the nays (not worth it, and why). No pitch. If none of it stacks up, you've lost an hour and a brew.

The questions everyone asks

Will it replace the software we already use?

Almost never. It sits between the systems you've got and removes the manual steps: the copying, the re-typing, the checking. Your booking system, your accounts package and your shop stay exactly where they are. If something genuinely needs replacing, you'll be told that on the look-round, not sold it later.

How long does it take?

The look-round is an hour. The half-day is four hours and you leave with something working. A build is typically four weeks from agreeing the price, and you see progress every week. Nothing takes "a quarter".

Who owns what gets built?

You do. The code, the accounts it runs under, the data, the prompts. If you stop working with us, it keeps running and you can hand it to anyone. We keep the right to reuse general methods, never anything specific to your business.

Where does our data go?

It stays in your systems. The AI reads what it's given permission to read, under business terms that exclude training, with everything logged. Nothing is copied into a consumer AI app, and nothing leaves the UK where the provider lets us choose. The customer data post has the long version.

What happens when it gets something wrong?

It will, occasionally, which is why every build has a point where a person checks before anything reaches a customer or touches money. The AI drafts, flags and summarises; a human approves. When it does get something wrong, the log shows exactly what it read and why, so it gets fixed rather than guessed at.

Will it replace our staff?

Nay. It replaces the part of their day that's typing the same thing into three systems. Every business we've done this in has the same people afterwards, doing more of the job they were hired for. If a tool's whole case is "then you can let someone go", we'd rather not build it.

Do we need to buy anything?

Usually a business-tier AI account at £20–30 a month per person who uses it, and that's often it. If a build needs a server or a service, it goes on an account you own and we tell you the running cost before starting. No "AI platform" subscriptions.

Do you work outside Harrogate?

Anywhere within an hour's drive for the look-round and the half-day, which covers most of North and West Yorkshire. Builds and support work anywhere, since most of it happens at a desk. For the bespoke and MCP work, location doesn't matter at all.

Step one is free and takes an hour. There's not much to lose.
