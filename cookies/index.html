Cookies

Last updated 27 October 2026

This site does not set cookies of its own, and it does not run analytics or advertising trackers. That's why there's no cookie banner.

What does load

  • Fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser requests the font files from Google, which sees your IP address in the process. No cookie is set.
  • The booking calendar, when it's embedded on the Book section, is provided by a third party and may set its own cookies to work. Its cookie policy applies within that box.
  • Hosting by Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie in some circumstances, for example to tell apart real visitors from automated traffic.

If we ever add analytics, this page and the site will change to ask for your consent first.
