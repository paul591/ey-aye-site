Accessibility

Last updated 27 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this site. It's built as plain HTML with real headings, real links and real form fields, so it works with keyboards, screen readers and browser zoom. Text and background colours are chosen to meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, and the site works on a phone without horizontal scrolling.

What we know isn't perfect

  • Some images are placeholders while the site is new; they'll get proper descriptions as they're added.
  • The booking calendar is a third-party embed and we can't change how accessible it is. If it doesn't work for you, email us and we'll book you in by hand.

Tell us

If something on this site is hard to use, email paul@ey-aye.co.uk and we'll fix it or find another way to get you what you need.
