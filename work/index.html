Loftsoft Racing · our own race-data system

"What's the camber at two degrees of roll?" Asked in plain English, answered from the data.

A data logger designed in-house for autograss and club racing, with a dashboard, a 3D suspension model, setup tools and lap-by-lap logs. On top of it, an assistant called Lofty that can read every channel, every session and every setup change, so a driver asks a question and gets an answer with the numbers behind it, and a view on what they mean. This is "one place to ask" at full stretch, and it's the system Paul runs on his own car.

Pit Wall · Purple Helmet's dashboard · one place to ask, built for ourselves

Shopify, Meta Ads, Klaviyo and the print costs, on one page, with an AI that reads it every Monday.

Four systems that never talked to each other, joined into a single page: net sales and orders, online versus trade stand, what each design actually makes after the printer's charges, paid versus organic, email flows, and a shared action plan. Every number says where it came from. On top of it, the "race engineer's notes": the AI reads the week's figures and writes down what to do next, which you can tick off.

No agency, no dashboard subscription, no wrapper. Standard models, the systems' own connectors, and the know-how we'll pass on. The same build, pointed at a clinic's bookings or a workshop's job sheet, is the morning brief.
